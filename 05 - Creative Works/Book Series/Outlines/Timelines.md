# Book Series Timeline (master) — Reading Pillars + Full Arc (Locked 2026-11-17)

## Reading Order (The Four Pillars)
- **Book 1 — Rebellion**: war-blood, betrayal, Fenris unmasked, brothers reconciled; hybrid equality forced on a dying kingdom.
- **Book 2 — Love + Politics**: relationships grow faster than wars break; hybrid/human bridge cemented by marriage (Alyra + Zaphrium); brothers reunite; charter established; Aldric dies.
- **Book 3 — Battle**: war on EVERY front — seven Realms and things walking out of the void all move at once. Aethoria barely survives. Alyra is the only thing keeping the centre from collapsing.
- **Book 4 — Aftermath**: the war is NOT done; Aethoria has only bought time. The unsettled world splits open again; Zaphrium assassinated; Lukas forced onto the throne by letter. The calm before the storm — left open for Book 5++.

---
## Phase 0 — The Rending (~17 years before present)
Lukas (9) and Fenris (8) are in the wheat fields when the Rift tears. Their mother burns herself holding the tear closed. The brothers are split by fate: Lukas into guard service, Fenris into captivity and Shadowfang conditioning.

---
## Book 1 — Twisted Destiny (~one year; the rebellion)
Lukas despised then celebrated; the Brotherhood rises in the Warrens; the Chimera is healed; the False Shard is unveiled; the Purge is broken; Aldric is forced to hybrid rights; the brothers reconcile; **Alyra ascends after the Spire Assault.** Lukas refuses the throne.

---
## Book 2 — Rise of Ascension (~6–12 months; THE LOVE BOOK + THE POLITICAL BOOK)
Two tracks run in parallel and they are inseparable: relationships, and the constitutional world they build.

### Love track (in order)
- **Zaphrium & Lukas**: raised together — dragon-sage and wolf-boy; now King-consort and warrior; intimate, bruising, formative.
- **Lukas & Fenris**: brothers reunited after Purge; tentative blood-coded trust; the family is re-forming.
- **Lukas & Nyssa**: tension; heat; growing romance under survival.
- **Alyra & Zaphrium**: politics that becomes love; Aurora voyage co-navigating; Dawn Court as the "Cold Reception"; treasury falls; **wedding at the table** at the moment the treaty is impromptu signed (Alyra chooses the Dawn Court location).
- **Fenris is present at the wedding — never seen**: he attends in shadow; Lukas faintly smells his presence; Zaphrium seems to know; no one else sees.

### Politics track
- Post-victory collapse; Aldric's illness.
- New Charter politicking; the Seven Realms watch; the Dawn Court brokers the Albanian-human union as constitutional shoring.
- Aldric dies (likely peaceful or with last breath blessing the union).
- Coronation of Alyra & Zaphrium as co-monarchs. Covenant sealed.

---
## Book 3 — Battle Grandeur (~next year; THE BATTLE BOOK)
Aethoria is fully committed to turning the multiversal Maw around — every faction, every foreign kingdom, every void-wounded thing that crawled through comes for them at once.

- **Multi-front war**: Vaelthorne (Shadow Mirror), Tagul wolves, Drune void-pursuers, Quor, Freya-mountain dead, Frey — plus things walking out of the Maw/multiverse. Chaos on every continent.
- **Alyra holds the human centre** from the throne; Zaphrium marches; Lukas's hybrid vanguard is the sharp spearhead.
- The Black Glass Tower looms as enemy sanctum; victory costs blood Zaphrium may never regain.
- **Multiversal Rift widens** each battle; aether junk falls from the sky; the old sky is not the sky anymore.
- **END OF BOOK 3**: retreat behind the Aegis; Zaphrium gravely wounded; Fenris debate-fracture; Aethoria alive but on its knees; the Seven Realms stirring; Alyra remains.

---
## Book 4 — Fall of Empires (THE AFTERMATH — calm before the storm)
- The war was not won; it was paused. The Six Realms are fractured countries; the Maw has not closed.
- **Zaphrium assassinated** by a rogue kingdom exploiting the Maw's spillover chaos / aether breach.
- **Alyra forced to marry Lukas** — Zaphrium's last covert plan, written in the letter, for hybrid/human preservation. They marry at the shattered centre of the Seven Realms.
- **Zaphrium's letter found** ("The Last Letter"): addressed to Lukas; commissions him into burden instead of caution; instructs the covenant marriage; ordains the law to fold the kingdoms back together.
- **Lukas crowned** at the end — forced into responsibility by letter, by Alyra's word, by the weight of the things he refused to carry.
- **Book ends unfinished**: Rifts multiply; new doors open elsewhere; Sept Realms fall apart; the world is still bleeding.
- NOT peace. Never was. A held breath.
- "The moon remembers" whispered across a healing Sik-apple end of the age.

---
## Book 5 — The Howling Shadow (parallel window, Fenris's cold biography)
Fenris's POV, colder and darker — the dark side of the same story (Bree Tanner-esque). Tracks childhood→Rending→Pits→Shadowfang rise→apocalypse window; ends with Fenris loose at the multiverse's door.
