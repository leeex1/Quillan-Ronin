# Song title : "Moonlight Mirage"

[Intro]
(Static fades in, low pulsing synth)
Yeah...
Just shadows and the streetlights.
Where do I begin?

[Verse 1]
Shadows stretch where the sodium fades
A city asleep, but my mind’s wide awake
A million footsteps in a lost parade
Neon signs whisper secrets in code
Heartbeats sync to the rhythm of the road
But the night hides truths I’ve never been told
I'm driving blind, nowhere left to go
Watching the colors bleed into the cold.

I see a ghost

Of what I fear most

But it’s not close...
