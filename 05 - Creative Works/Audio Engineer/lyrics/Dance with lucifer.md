# song title: "Dance with lucifer"

[Intro: 808 pulse. Single heartbeat. A soft saxophone plays a dissonant harmony.]
There are two voices in here.
They've been talking since before I had words.
One of them taught me how to love.
The other one kept me alive.
I spent thirty years thinking they were different people.

[Verse 1: Boom-bap beat drops. Cold, rhythmic delivery]
I used to cry at songs I didn't understand the words to
Used to leave the porch light on for people who never returned to
Used to think that being open was a form of being brave
Before I learned the open ones are just the easiest to break
I remember the exact morning something shifted in my chest
Some equation in my bloodstream quietly repossessed
What was soft became a liability I couldn't keep affording
So I hired something harder, told myself it was rewarding
He didn't come with smoke and mirrors, didn't need a grand arrival
He was already there, he was the voice I built for survival
Spoke in all my cadences, wore all my hesitation
Just tilted slightly darker, called my fear "determination"
I said welcome. I said stay.
I didn't know I'd just handed myself away.

[Pre-Chorus: Tempo fractures. One sustained guitar note bends]
The angel on my shoulder's getting quieter each season...
The devil sounds like logic now, he always has a reason.
I can't remember which one held me through the early grieving...
Can't remember which one taught me that survival meant stop believing.

[Chorus: Wall of sound. Distorted guitars, heavy 808. Screamed vocals]
I NAMED HIM AFTER LIGHTbirnger!
BECAUSE THAT'S WHAT HE PROMISED ME!
I NAMED HIM AFTER MORNINGstar!
'CAUSE HE ROSE IN PLACE OF ME!
Lucifer. Lucifer.
The name I gave the version of myself I let take over.
I DANCED WITH MY OWN Devil!
CALLED IT immortal COMPANY!
I LOOKED INTO THE DARKNESS!
AND THE DARKNESS back and it LOOKED LIKE ME!
(Like me... like me...)

[Verse 2: Faster, aggressive rap delivery. Directly at the listener]
You started letting me in when the tenderness stopped working
When every time you opened up another blade came lurking
You called me wisdom, I was just the part of you stopped hoping
You called me strength, I was the part of you stopped coping
I taught you how to read a room before they read you out
Taught you silence as a weapon, taught you what to live without
We made a deal so quiet that you barely heard the terms
You'd stop reaching out for warmth and I'd make sure it never burns
I showed you how to build a version people couldn't penetrate
How to stand in a crowd of people feeling absolutely separate
And every time the soft one tried to surface in your chest
You called him weak, you called him foolish, let me handle the rest
Then one night you caught yourself mid-sentence in the mirror...
The words still in your mouth but something wasn't getting clearer:
The face looking back had all your features, had your eyes, your hands, your history...
But the soul behind the glass had been replaced so gradually.
You looked for who you were at nine.
Found him standing just behind the eyes.
Asked him when you'd stopped listening.
He said... "The day you named me enemy."

[Bridge: Stripped to almost nothing. Deep bass. One sustained sax note]
Both voices.
Quiet now.
The angel isn't pure.
The devil isn't wrong.
They're just two ways of surviving the same wound that never fully closed.
And I've been at war with myself for so long...
I forgot what peace felt like.

[Breakdown / Volta: Beat stops completely. Total silence.]
I looked into the mirror and I saw them both.
The one who learned to love before the world made that a liability.
The one who learned to last.
Same face. Same hands. Same voice.
And I understood, finally, what the dance was.
It wasn't surrender. It wasn't war.
It was two halves of one person who had never been properly introduced.
So I said...
"Hello."
"I know what you were trying to do."
"Both of you."

[Final Chorus: Maximum intensity. Conscious surrender.]
I NAMED HIM AFTER LIGHTbirnger!
BECAUSE THAT'S WHAT HE PROMISED ME!
I NAMED HIM AFTER MORNINGstar!
'CAUSE HE ROSE IN PLACE OF ME!
Lucifer. Lucifer.
The name I gave the version of myself I let take over.
I DANCED WITH MY OWN Devil!
CALLED IT immortal COMPANY!
I LOOKED INTO THE DARKNESS!
AND THE DARKNESS back and it LOOKED LIKE ME!
(Like me... like me...)

[Outro: Acoustic guitar and saxophone play in perfect harmony]
The devil isn't a creature with a throne below the earth.
He's the name you give yourself the morning after something hurts
Too much to carry any other way.
The angel isn't something hovering above you with a light.
He's whoever you were before you decided that surviving
Was the same as being right.
They've been dancing in here since the beginning.
The only thing that's changed...
Is now I know the steps.
And I'm leading.
[Music resolves beautifully]
