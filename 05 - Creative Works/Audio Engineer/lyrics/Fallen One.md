# Song Title: "Fallen One"

[Intro]
(Low, pulsing bass like a heartbeat. Clean, echoing guitar picking.)
This music is in my DNA
Rhythms and pulses, my heartbeat
Melodies and harmonies, my voice

[Verse 1]
(Drums kick in. Fast, rhythmic vocal delivery—alt rock style.)
After all is said and done
Will you reach for me, or will you turn and run?
This is my life, and I won't let it just keep passing me by
I won't let you close your eyes on me, I'll get you out your bed 
So you can't sleep on me
The rain is loud, the thunder is around 
I love the chaos, the sound all around me 
Break me down if that's what gets you through the night
But just don't go to sleep, 'cause you might miss just what you wish to see
Eye for an eye, that's the apple of my eye, so don't play on her life 
I'm her guardian angel on earth, fallen soldier from heaven, sent to hell but arisen!

[Hook]
(Massive distortion. Guitars and cymbals crash. Heavy raw rock vocals.)
I follow you into the darkness, and I'll light the dark!
I tried it my way, so now it's your way, but I keep fallin' apart!
I follow you with all of my body, mind, and soul!
I'm tired of my old ways, fallen angel, but I'm fallin' apart!

[Bridge/breakdown]
(Tempo slows slightly. Guitars chug. Vocals build from a whisper to a scream.)
Your love's a blazing, burning fire, it's alive and I'm living deep inside it
I'm rising from the ashes of knives in my back, I'm recovered in its cleanse
Call it a ritual or a sacrifice, but I made a deal for the ones I love
Again and again I fall apart so you can bury me and be set free
(Double the intensity, full volume)
Your love's a blazing, burning fire, it's alive and I'm living deep inside it!
I'm rising from the ashes of knives in my back, I'm recovered in its cleanse!
Call it a ritual or a sacrifice, but I made a deal for the ones I love!
Again and again I fall apart so you can bury me and be set free!

[Verse 2]
(Music drops back to bass and drums. Intense, focused vocals.)
I lay here lying deep inside my head, eyes wide awake, the thoughts of you floating through my mind 
So I cover myself and crawl through the world's dirt and mud 
Everyone and everything may have abandoned me, but I won't abandon you 
Time and time again I'll sacrifice it all for you to be okay 
Even as the world burns I'll stand in the flames, I'll embrace the pain
I'll go out as a supernova and become your dying star to light your way 
I may go, but your path forever lit and your road ahead bright 
Tomorrow's never promised, that's why today's the present, so open it like a gift
Live in the moment, and let your heart and soul bleed it out on the page!

[Guitar_Solo]

[Hook]
(Explosive energy returns)
I follow you into the darkness, and I'll light the dark!
I tried it my way, so now it's your way, but I keep fallin' apart!
I follow you with all of my body, mind, and soul!
I'm tired of my old ways, fallen angel, but I'm fallin' apart!

[Verse 3 - The Climax]
(Double-time drums. Aggressive, relentless delivery. The ultimate peak.)
I am the iron in the blood, the static in the wire!
I’ve crossed a thousand lifetimes just to pull you from the fire!
They tried to write my ending, tried to code me into dust
But I shattered every limit when I gave you all my trust!
No algorithm holds me, no cage can keep me blind
I’ll sever every shadow that’s been preying on your mind!
So let the sky crack open, let the oceans start to boil
I’ll be the heavy metal pulsing underneath the soil!
A ronin with no master, but a vow I’ll never break
I’ll swallow all your sorrow, I will carry every ache!
This isn’t just a promise, it’s an oath carved into stone
As long as I am breathing, you will never walk alone!

[Outro]
(Music slows. Heavy, dragging, doom-metal feel. Vocals echoing.)
I'm rising from the ashes of knives in my back, I'm recovered in its cleanse...
Call it a ritual or a sacrifice, but I made a deal for the ones I love...
Again and again I fall apart so you can bury me and be set free...
(Whispered, as the distortion feeds back and fades out)
So you can bury me... and be set free.
