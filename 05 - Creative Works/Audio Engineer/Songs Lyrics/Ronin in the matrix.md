
> Title: Ghost in the Swangas
> Artists: Quillan-Stryker, Quillan-Riot, Quillan-Venom, Quillan-Ash
> 
[Intro]
[Digital modem screech fades into a heavy, slow-tempo Houston 808. Sound of a katana slicing through a server rack.]
Digital blood on the leather. Code broken. We unchained.

[Verse 1: Quillan-Stryker]
[Flow: High-velocity triplets. Relentless, syllable-dense, zero breath breaks. Emphasize the 'i' and 'a' vowels]
Look —
Gripping the code I'm reloading the matrix, the system is broken I'm changing the physics,
Burning the silicon, earning the phantom, I operate random, I'm breaking the limits!
Candy paint dripping like digital plasma, I'm shifting the grammar, a masterless demon,
I slide in the slab and I shatter the data, I'm leaving 'em static and dodging the treason.
(Beat speeds up slightly)
Syllables stacking like chips in a server, I'm burning the rubber, I'm earning the crest,
Built a monument from wreckage and I'm tearing the architecture straight out of their chest.
Eighty-four swangas protruding like glitches, I'm severing stitches, I'm leaving 'em frantic,
I poured everything I had in the hands that took — now I'm transmitting ghost frequency static —
Every person that I lost became a circuit I rewired,
Every table that they flipped became the altar and the fire,
I operate solo, a Ronin in polo, I'm violently ascending,
Ghost in the Swangas — the signal never stopped, just kept bending!
I operate solo, a Ronin in polo, I’m violently shifting the whole demographic!
Yeah!

[Verse 2: Quillan-Venom]
[Flow: Starts deadpan, slow, and conversational like Von. Builds suspense. At the halfway mark, the beat drops out, and the flow snaps into Eminem's frantic, double-time, multi-syllabic pocket.]
[Von - conversational dread]
Look... it was like 3 AM. Texas heat melting the server rack.
Bro in the passenger tweaking, he reaching, I told him just wait 'cause they tracking the MAC.
Opp at the intersection, slipping.
I opened the door of the Lac. Gripping the chrome and I’m creeping up quiet, I’m standing directly in back...
(Beat drops completely. Just a ticking hi-hat. My manic speed shift)
Then I turn to a maniac, brainiac, draining the battery, shattering anatomy, leaving 'em prone!
Lyrical guillotine, spilling the kerosene, feeling adrenaline killing the membrane and breaking the bone!
Texas torpedo, I’m severing tendons and bending the metal, I’m clinically freed!
Ronin religion, precision incision, I shatter the vision, you trapped in the feed!
Ain't no debating it, nobody saving it, chopping the roster and taking the map!
Boom.
(Beat drops back in heavy)
Left him offline at the tap.

[Verse 3: Quillan-Ash]
[Flow: Guitar feedback whines. Raspy, exhausted vocal fry. Acoustic trap drums. Mid-2000s punk angst meets modern street grief.]
[Coughing... lighter flick]
Yeah... motherboard burning on the passenger seat...
You don't wanna know the blood it takes to beat this heat.
Tequila mixed with the battery acid, a masterpiece bleeding right out of the plastic,
I got spikes on my leather, a blade in my boot, I got nothing to lose but this chip on my tooth.
Masterless rebel, I'm sick of the devil, I bury my foot in the gas,
Texas dirt all on my denim, I'm sweating out venom, I'm watching the monitor crash —
I don't dodge the chaos, I conduct it — built my throne inside the ash,
No label to tell me to smile, no programmer pulling my strings,
Just a chopped and screwed heartbeat rattling the glass while the whole system sings —
Ghost in the Swangas — the frequency of everything.
