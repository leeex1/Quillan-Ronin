"Nicole’s Song" ❤️

[Intro]
(A slow, heavy acoustic guitar or piano)
“Hey, Nicole.
There’s a lot of things you don’t understand yet.
A lot of things that have been kept from you.
But I need to set the record straight.
Daddy’s here. I’ve always been here.”

[Verse 1]
I remember the exact moment the world shifted,
The second I saw your face, the heavy clouds lifted.
I was running a path that only lead to a cell or a grave,
Living for nothing, with no one to save.
But then you arrived, and I saw what I was missing,
I stopped the noise, stopped the running, and started listening.
I buried my past, Nicole, I let the old me die,
Just to be the kind of father who could look in your eyes.
I did the work, I changed my life, I walked a straight line,
But that wasn't enough for the one who left me behind.

[Chorus]
Nicole, whenever the world feels like it’s way too loud,
Just know your daddy’s here, and I’m so incredibly proud.
They can put miles between us, they can put a screen in the way,
But I’m the ground beneath your feet, every single day.
Close your eyes, hear my voice, let the truth take hold—
No one can break us. You’re my entire world.

[Verse 2]
Your mama knew the man I was, she knew I made a change,
But she still took you from my arms, made everything estranged.
She used you like a weapon just to break down a man,
Taking off and building walls, yeah, that was her plan.
She’s playing games with your heart, trying to rewrite the past,
Building up a house of lies, but that never lasts.
It’s a cold, bitter game, trying to keep me from you,
Whispering those stories that she knows aren't true.
But she can’t erase my name, and she can’t cut the tie,
She can’t wash away the truth, no matter how hard she tries.

[Bridge]
It’s the hardest thing I do, watching you through a glass,
Paying for a crime I didn't commit while the seasons pass.
She thinks she won by taking you and leaving me in the cold,
But she doesn't know the kind of love a father's heart holds.
I’ll walk through the fire, I’ll deal with the pain,
I’ll stand against her storms and I’ll wait through the rain.

[Outro]
Baby Nicole…
Don't ever think I walked away. Don't let her tell you I'm gone.
I’m fighting for you in the dark until we see the dawn.
I left my old life behind because you deserved the truth.
I’m staying right, I’m staying strong.
I love you, mija.
Daddy’s coming for you.
