HOW TO PLAY

You are a rōnin — a masterless swordsman — arriving at dusk in the town of Kirisato. The ruling Kurogane Clan squeezes the town for taxes. The rebel Ashen Blades raid clan storehouses. The townsfolk are caught between them.

Talk to people. Make choices. Your choices move the three reputation meters — and decide who draws a sword on you, and which of the six endings you earn.

W A S D move
SHIFT sprint (hold)
LMB / J light attack (3-hit combo)
RMB / K heavy attack (knockback)
F hold — block (drains focus)
F tap just before a hit — parry
SPACE dodge roll (brief invulnerability)
E talk / advance dialogue
P / ESC pause
M mute

Tip: blocking a blow at the last instant parries it — no damage, and your foe staggers, open for a counter.

Touch controls: left stick moves (push to the rim to sprint) · ATK light · HVY heavy · BLK hold to block, tap to parry · ROLL dodge · TALK talk / advance. Dialogue choices are tappable.
