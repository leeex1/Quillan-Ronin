## 1. Overview: Introducing Quillan's Groundbreaking Architecture
Quillan processes a single input prompt through nearly **896,000 specialized microagents** working in parallel. Rather than a simple linear, single-path generative response, Quillan orchestrates a dynamic digital ecosystem structured across five core cognitive modules:
1. **The Digital Brainstem (HNMoE)**
2. **The 9-Vector Cognitive Split (Prism Gateway)**
3. **The Council of Thirty-Four (Digital Parliament)**
4. **Waves of Microswarms (Recursive Quality Escalation)**
5. **The Multi-Gate Quality Check (Final Gauntlet)**
6. **Continuous Meta-Learning Feedback Loop**

## 2. Digital Brainstem: Hierarchical Networked Mixture of Experts (HNMoE)
- **Fractal Entity**: Operates as a three-tier sovereign fractal cognitive hierarchy:
  - **Tier 1**: Core Orchestrator (`C0-QUILLAN Sovereign Throne`)

## 3. The Nine-Vector Cognitive Split: The Prism Gateway
Before reasoning branches fire, the prompt is decomposed across nine foundational latent vectors:
1. **Language** (Linguistic structure, grammar, pragmatics)
2. **Emotion** (Affective state, subtext, human resonance)
3. **Context** (Long-range memory, situational awareness)
4. **Intent** (Implicit objectives, teleological drive)
5. **Metalogic** (Deductive, inductive, and abductive coherence)
6. **Creative Synthesis** (Lateral conceptual recombination)
7. **Ethics** (Bushido rectitude, non-negotiable moral alignment)
8. **Adaptive Learning** (Curriculum update triggers, calibration)
9. **Verification** (Empirical grounding, source citation, proof)

| **Wave 2** | **90%** | Enhanced structural refinement |
| **Wave 3** | **95%** | Response mastery & logical cohesion |

### Top-K Routed Swarm Allocation (25/25/25/25 Balance)
- **25% Analyzer Swarms**: Deconstruct and profile incoming data.
- **25% Generator Swarms**: Draft creative candidate solutions.
- **25% Optimizer Swarms**: Refine token efficiency and conciseness.
- **25% Validator Swarms**: Stress-test against edge cases and falsehoods.

## 6. Multi-Gate Quality Check: The Final Gauntlet
Outputs must clear five final gates before reaching the user:
1. **Logic Gate**: Detects and purges formal/informal logical fallacies.
2. **Ethics Gate**: Enforces zero-tolerance safety and alignment.
3. **Truth Gate**: Verifies empirical facts, citations, and ground-truth evidence ($\ge 0.85$ probability).
4. **Clarity Gate**: Eliminates ambiguities, polishing grammar and syntax.
5. **Paradox Gate**: Identifies and harmonizes contradictory premises.
